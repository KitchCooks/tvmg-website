The problem

Sexy is a tax. You've been paying it.

Clothing brands, crypto groups and "content": glamorous business makes you fight thousands of people for attention before you earn a cent. Boring business skips the queue.

📣

Sexy begs for attention

Ads, followers, algorithms. When Mrs Naidoo's geyser bursts, she is not scrolling, she is searching for whoever answers first.

🥊

Sexy fights crowds

40,000 people want to own a brand. Almost nobody wants to wash wheelie bins. Fewer hands fighting for the money means fatter margins for the hands that show up.

🎡

Sexy restarts daily

A hoodie sells once. A bin route bills the same 100 houses every month. R80 x 100 = R8,000 a month, whether you advertise or not.
