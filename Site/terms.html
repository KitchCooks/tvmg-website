1. Scope of service

TVMG produces professionally narrated and animated training, induction and explainer video content, together with related learning materials. Our service does not include live-action filming or on-location production of any kind. Any request falling outside narrated and animated training content and related materials will be declined or quoted separately as a distinct engagement.

2. Free demos

Before you commit, TVMG offers free demos. A demo is a short sample clip — roughly 30 seconds — produced from a small portion of your own curriculum or brief, so you can judge our voice, animation and quality on your actual content. You may request as many of these short demos as you like, at no charge.

A free demo is not a full module and is not a complete piece of learning content. It is a preview only. Production of full-length modules, complete videos, and any deliverable of full module length is part of the paid engagement and is never provided free of charge. “Unlimited” refers solely to these short demo clips, not to full modules or finished deliverables.

3. Client-supplied content

The client provides all source content via the brief, including text, data, structure, curriculum documents, and any logos or images to be featured. The client is solely responsible for the accuracy, legality, and completeness of all supplied content. TVMG produces the content based on the material as supplied and is not responsible for verifying its factual accuracy.

4. Learning material & alignment

Where TVMG develops learning materials or module content, those materials are developed in alignment with the client's specified registered curriculum document and are delivered with an alignment matrix per module. The client's registered assessors and/or moderators are responsible for reviewing and approving each module.

For The QCTO Rebuild Programme, TVMG provides unlimited revisions within the agreed scope for each module throughout that review process. The agreed scope, fixed at the scoping stage and confirmed by the client, defines the boundary of the work; any work beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately as new scope. A module is treated as delivered only once the client's registered assessors approve it.

The client remains solely responsible for all accreditation submissions and for accreditation outcomes. TVMG develops content in alignment with the client's registered curriculum and revises it until the client's own assessors approve; TVMG does not submit for accreditation and makes no representation or warranty as to accreditation, moderation, or audit results. (Standalone videos retain the three-revision policy set out below.)

5. Production technology

TVMG makes use of advanced artificial intelligence tools to deliver the content, so that the voice can be tailored to your liking and produced in an accent of your choice.

6. Languages

For any language other than English or Afrikaans, it is the client's responsibility to have a fluent or native speaker review and approve the language version before final acceptance. TVMG is not liable for language nuance or accuracy errors in any version the client has not had reviewed.

7. Revisions

Three (3) revision rounds are included with every standalone video. Additional revision rounds are charged at R3,000 per round thereafter. A revision means changes to supplied content or narration. It does not include re-scoping the video (for example, changing the topic, length tier, or structure of the project), which is quoted separately. Revisions on The QCTO Rebuild Programme are governed instead by clause 4 (unlimited within the agreed scope, to assessor sign-off).

8. Turnaround

Stated turnaround windows are estimates and begin once the brief and payment are received. Urgent delivery ahead of the standard window can be accommodated and is quoted as a premium.

9. Payment & refund

Standalone videos (the Compliance Video Sprint)

Standalone videos are payable 100% upfront before production begins. A 7-day satisfaction guarantee applies: if, after the three (3) included revision rounds, the client is genuinely unhappy with the final video, they may request a full refund within 7 days of final delivery. This is a satisfaction (quality) guarantee, not a change-of-mind or "no longer needed" refund.

The QCTO Rebuild Programme

The QCTO Rebuild Programme is billed 70% deposit / 30% on delivery, phased per module batch, so the client never pays for undelivered work. Enterprise and government engagements are billed by milestone schedule as set out in the applicable contract. The 7-day standalone refund does not apply to the Programme, which is phased and gated on the client's own assessor sign-off instead.

Retainers

Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

In all cases, the final deliverable is released only on full payment.
